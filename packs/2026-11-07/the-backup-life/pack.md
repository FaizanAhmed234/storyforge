**Opening hook text:** I was only built to die for him.

```
For my whole existence, I slept in the dark, waiting for my primary to bleed. But yesterday, the monitor flatlined permanently, leaving me awake in a world that was never meant to be mine. What happens when the spare finally has to live?

If you were the backup, would you try to play the lead?
```
