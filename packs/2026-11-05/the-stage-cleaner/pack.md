# The Stage Cleaner: A Scripted Tragedy

**Opening hook text:** She doesn't clean scenes, she creates them.

## Caption (tap copy)

```
I spent years scrubbing floors, believing I was erasing sins. I never realized the bleach was just a primer for the nightmares I was ordered to plant. Is destiny something we face, or something we are staged to fulfill?
```
