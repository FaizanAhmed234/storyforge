# The Permanent Pause

**Opening hook text:** What if your words had an expiration date?

## Caption (tap copy)

```
Arthur thought his new smartwatch was tracking his health. Instead, it was counting down every syllable he had left. When the script runs out, silence takes over forever.

Would you spend your remaining words wisely, or stay silent?
```
