# The Compliance Scent

**Opening hook text:** Everyone agreed. That was the nightmare.

## Caption (tap copy)

```
She wanted a fragrance that commanded absolute obedience from everyone in the room. But when every single person started smiling and nodding to her every word, she realized the terrifying truth: the scent hadn't changed their minds. It had just silenced their dissent. What is a victory worth if nobody pushes back?
```
