# The Birthday Loop

**Opening hook text:** 10,000 days of the exact same lie.

## Caption (tap copy)

```
Ten thousand repetitions. Every sunrise, every word, every breakfast cooked by the woman who raised him. Until today, when she looked at him and didn't know his name. 

How do you break a loop when the script is suddenly rewritten?
```
