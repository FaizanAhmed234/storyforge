# The Stolen Silhouettes

**Opening hook text:** Three shadows. Two people. One impossible truth.

## Caption (tap copy)

```
Mom always said we were inseparable, walking together under the flickering streetlights of a rain-slicked city. But when I finally looked down at the wet pavement and counted the darkness stretching behind us, I realized I was never meant to be alive at all. What is truly keeping you tethered to this world?
```
